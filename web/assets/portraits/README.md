# Illustration provenance

The repository contains three image assets, all generated for this project with the built-in image generation tool on September 8, 2026. They depict fictional characters. No photographic portraits, stock photographs, externally hosted images, or downloaded fonts are included. The two unused photographic generations were removed from the working tree; earlier Git commits still contain them.

These are the recorded generation prompts, preserved as provenance. Style references describe the requests and do not imply affiliation with a studio. Repository license selection remains open; this inventory is not a separate asset license.

## Astrid

`astrid-illustrated.png` is the fictional illustrated face of Astrid, the AI matchmaking friend.

Generation prompt:

> Create a square illustrated avatar for Astrid, an AI matchmaking friend. A wholly fictional young adult woman about 25, Mediterranean / Levantine appearance, warm olive-brown skin, dark expressive almond-shaped eyes, thick brows, a short softly tousled black curly bob and a playful, knowing smile. She must look distinctly like a drawn character, not a photograph, not a realistic painting. Charming Studio Ghibli-inspired hand-drawn animation aesthetic: clear ink contours, simplified appealing face, flat cel shading with gentle watercolor texture, a few tiny whimsical golden sparkles. Moss-green casual overshirt over a warm ivory shirt. Head and shoulders, face centrally composed and legible at small circular avatar size, generous margins above head, muted pale parchment and sage background with a softly suggested leaf shape. Cozy, youthful, spirited, perceptive, approachable. Avoid pink, hearts, photoreal skin texture, glamour makeup, long brown wavy hair, photo lighting, corporate branding. No text, no logos, no watermark. A clean finished character illustration for a calm nature-inspired storybook chat interface.

## Clubhouse

`../clubhouse/cupid-retired.png` depicts a theatrical, non-graphic fallen Cupid and original fictional partygoers.

Generation prompt:

> Create a wide 3:2 hand-drawn animated storybook poster illustration for a strange, funny matchmaking clubhouse called Astrid. No lettering anywhere; the website will place text separately. The central scene: Cupid is dead, comically and non-graphically. Cupid is an unmistakably ADULT small winged man in a rumpled pale tunic, sprawled face-up on a mossy chaise lounge with cartoon X eyes, tiny crooked halo, a limp feathered wing, and a snapped heart-shaped bow beside him. No blood, wounds or gore. He looks like a theatrical fallen love god. Around him an eccentric cast of ORIGINAL adult fictional characters calmly mingle at a ramshackle forest singles party: a suave lanky vampire holding a teacup, a shy moss-covered knight with flowers growing from his helmet, and a glamorous witch in oversized boots checking a tiny flip phone. A little long-legged black cat steals Cupid's arrow. Mischievous rather than scary. Whimsical classic Japanese hand-drawn animation feel with tactile watercolor backgrounds, bold readable cel-shaded characters, confident ink lines, comic storytelling, unexpected details. Palette moss green, acid pear yellow, parchment, vermilion accents, deep ink purple. Composition big expressive characters grouped low/center with generous calm pale parchment space at upper left for website copy. Charming, absurd, memorable; absolutely not corporate, not romantic pink, not a therapy illustration. No text, no watermark, no UI.

## Reusable participant portraits

`cast-illustrated.png` is a 2×2 sheet of four fictional adult characters. CSS crops one quadrant per profile. The original fixture assignments are Maya top left, Eli top right, Theo bottom left and Elena bottom right; newly created profiles reuse these illustrations. Names, ages, occupations and interests in the art direction are not evidence about a participant. Live profile facts come from that participant's conversation or edits.

Generation prompt:

> Create a single square 2-by-2 character portrait contact sheet for a fictional matchmaking clubhouse demo, four exactly equal square panels, no gutters, no lettering. Ghibli-inspired hand-drawn animation, expressive clean ink lines, warm cel shading, textured watercolor scenery. All four are unmistakably ADULT people in their mid twenties, youthful adult faces, NOT children, NOT photorealistic. Head and shoulders, every face centered within its own quadrant with generous headroom for circular avatar crop. Top left Maya, 25: dark brown skin, voluminous tightly curly hair gathered high in a loose puff, big round amber glasses, rust-orange oversized sweater, laughing crooked smile, ceramics studio background. Top right Eli, 27: warm brown skin, shaggy wavy black hair, clean shaven, long face, narrow expressive eyes, cobalt blue chore jacket over mustard shirt, bookish mischievous grin, shelves of books background. Bottom left Theo, 26: fair freckled skin, very short bleached buzzcut, one small hoop earring, broad angular face, forest green hiking jacket, confident relaxed smile, breezy mountains background. Bottom right Elena, 24: light golden skin, sharp straight jet-black chin-length bob with blunt fringe, small dark eyes, red workwear jacket, wry half-smile, old cinema posters and warm projector glow in background. Four very DIFFERENT facial silhouettes, palettes, hair, and personal styles. Appealing and imperfect, like an interesting group of friends from a beautifully animated film. No hearts, text, watermark, borders, logos or extra faces. Exact 2x2 even layout.
